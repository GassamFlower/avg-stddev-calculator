How to Calculate Average and Standard Deviation

Average (Mean)

The average is the sum of all values divided by the number of values:

x̄ = (Σxᵢ) / n

Standard Deviation

Standard deviation measures how spread out the data is from the mean.

Sample Standard Deviation

Use this when your data is a sample from a larger population:

s = √[ Σ(xᵢ - x̄)² / (n - 1) ]

Population Standard Deviation

Use this when your data represents the entire population:

σ = √[ Σ(xᵢ - μ)² / N ]

When to Use Each

  • Sample standard deviation (s): Use when analyzing a subset of data to estimate the variability of a larger population. This is the most common case in research and quality control.
  • Population standard deviation (σ): Use when you have data for every member of the population you're studying, such as test scores for an entire class.
